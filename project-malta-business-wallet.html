Malta Business Wallet Launch & Promo Videos

A three-video launch for Malta Business Wallet, shaped around clear product communication, confident pacing, and motion-led presentation.

Video Production Motion Design

Role & Context

Created during my role at Grant Thornton Malta, this project focused on producing three launch and promotional videos for Malta Business Wallet.

Launch Videos

Description

This project presents Malta Business Wallet through a set of three launch and promotional videos, translating the service into concise visual stories for audiences encountering it for the first time.

The work focuses on sequencing, visual clarity, motion rhythm, and brand-consistent presentation so each video feels direct, polished, and easy to follow across digital channels.
