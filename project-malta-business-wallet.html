Overview

Turning a launch message into clear video communication

Created during my role at Grant Thornton Malta, this project focused on editing supplied visuals into three launch and promotional videos for Malta Business Wallet. The goal was to introduce the service through concise, polished videos that could be understood quickly by audiences encountering it for the first time.

The strength of the project is in structure and presentation rather than a single static visual. Using Premiere Pro and After Effects, the work focused on sequencing, timing, motion emphasis, and brand-consistent presentation so the message felt direct and easy to follow across LinkedIn, national TV, and the Malta Business Wallet website.

Communication Approach

What the videos needed to do

01

Introduce Quickly

Each video needed to make the product message approachable without requiring extra explanation from the viewer.

02

Keep The Pace Clear

The editing and scene order had to support quick comprehension while still feeling confident and polished.

03

Stay On Brand

The supplied visuals had to be assembled and refined into a launch asset that felt consistent across social, broadcast, and website contexts.

Reflection

What this project demonstrates

As a portfolio piece, Malta Business Wallet shows my ability to support a professional launch through visual editing, structured pacing, and polished motion-led presentation. It sits between design and production: the work is not only about making the videos look good, but about helping a viewer understand the product message quickly.

If expanding the case study further, the most useful additions would be edit frames or short snippets from the final videos that show how the supplied visuals were paced, transitioned, and prepared for different channels.
